Who We Are

About RapidFast

An independent AI and tech content platform built for students, developers, and early-career professionals

Our Story

RapidFast started with a straightforward problem: keeping up with AI in 2025 felt like drinking from a fire hose. Every week brought a new model, a new tool, a new internship opportunity — and no single place covered all of it clearly and honestly, especially for people who are still building their skills.

Founded in 2025 and based in Bangalore, India, RapidFast is built by a small, focused team of technology writers and AI enthusiasts. We cover AI model updates, tool reviews, developer career guides, and tech hiring opportunities — written specifically for students and early-career professionals in India and beyond.

We don't chase pageviews or write clickbait. We write the kind of content we wish we'd had when we were starting out — clear, accurate, and genuinely helpful. Everything we publish is verified and written by people who actively follow the field they're covering.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To give students and early-career builders clear, honest, and actionable coverage of AI tools, model updates, and tech hiring — without the hype or the filler.

Our Vision

To become a trusted independent voice on AI and technology for the next generation of Indian developers, researchers, and builders entering the global tech economy.

What We Cover

We focus on topics that matter to people building careers in technology and AI:

AI Model Updates

In-depth coverage of major model releases — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok — explaining what actually changed and who it matters to.

AI Tool Reviews

Hands-on reviews of AI writing, coding, design, and productivity tools — tested by our team, not just summarized from press releases.

Career & Internship Guides

Practical breakdowns of AI and tech internship opportunities: what the role actually involves, what skills are needed, and how to apply successfully.

Tech How-To Guides

Step-by-step guides for developers: building apps with AI, using developer tools, deploying projects, and getting started with new technologies.

Our Editorial Standards

Every article on RapidFast is produced under a clear set of standards:

  • ✔ Verified — Facts are checked against primary sources before publishing
  • ✔ Original — All content is written by our team, not copied or scraped
  • ✔ Transparent — Affiliate relationships and sponsored content are clearly disclosed
  • ✔ Updated — Articles are revised when the underlying information changes
  • ✔ Accessible — Everything we publish is free, with no paywall

For full details on how we create and review content, see our Editorial Policy.

Our Values

The best technology coverage is honest about what works, what doesn't, and who it's actually for. We don't sensationalize, we don't over-hype, and we don't publish articles that exist purely to rank on search engines. If a tool isn't worth using, we say so.

We're based in India and proud of it. A significant portion of our content is written with the Indian tech job market and student experience in mind — covering the internships, tools, and opportunities most relevant to builders in this region.
