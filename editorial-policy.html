Editorial Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

Our Commitment: Every article, review, and guide published on RapidFast is created to genuinely help our readers — not to game search engines, push affiliate sales, or generate clicks. We hold ourselves to the same standard we'd want from a trusted colleague.

1. Who We Are

RapidFast is an independent technology and AI content platform based in Bangalore, India. We cover AI model updates, tool reviews, developer career guides, and internship opportunities — primarily for students, developers, and early-career professionals in India and beyond.

Our editorial work is carried out by writers and researchers who actively use the tools and follow the industry they write about. We do not publish content from writers who have no real-world experience with the topic.

2. Content Standards

Accuracy First

We publish only information we can verify. This means:

  • Claims about AI model capabilities are drawn from official documentation, hands-on testing, or reputable benchmarks
  • Salary ranges and hiring details come from official job postings, not speculation
  • We clearly distinguish between what we have personally tested and what we report from third-party sources

Original Reporting

All content on RapidFast is original. We do not republish, paraphrase, or scrape content from other publications. When we cite external sources, we link to them directly and credit them appropriately. Summaries of official announcements or research are clearly attributed.

Timeliness and Updates

The AI industry changes fast. When information we've published becomes outdated, we update the article and note the revision date at the top. We do not silently delete or rewrite articles in ways that misrepresent what was originally said.

3. Our Content Creation Process

  1. Topic Selection — We choose topics based on genuine reader interest, industry relevance, and our team's expertise. We do not create content purely for SEO volume if the topic has no real reader value.
  2. Research — We consult primary sources: official product pages, release notes, developer documentation, benchmark reports, and public data. For hiring guides, we review the actual job postings.
  3. Writing — Content is written by team members with relevant knowledge. We avoid filler, keyword-stuffing, and padded content that wastes the reader's time.
  4. Internal Review — Before publishing, articles are reviewed for factual accuracy, clarity, and completeness.
  5. Publishing — We include publish dates, and update dates when articles are revised, so readers know how current the content is.
  6. Post-publish Monitoring — We monitor articles for reader feedback and update them when the underlying information changes.

4. Reviews and Recommendations

When we review an AI tool, writing assistant, or developer product, we aim to give readers a complete, honest picture — including what works well and what doesn't. Our reviews are based on actual use, not marketing materials.

  • We do not accept payment in exchange for positive reviews
  • Sponsored content, if any, is clearly labeled as such at the top of the article
  • Affiliate relationships do not change how we evaluate or describe a product — we only recommend things we genuinely consider useful

5. Corrections and Retractions

We take accuracy seriously. If you spot an error in any of our articles, please contact us using the information below. We will:

  • Investigate the reported error promptly
  • Correct factual inaccuracies and note the correction at the bottom of the article
  • Issue a retraction if an article is found to be substantially incorrect

We do not alter published articles to hide errors without noting the change.

6. Conflicts of Interest

We disclose any relationship that could reasonably influence how we cover a topic. This includes:

  • Affiliate Links: Some links on our site may earn us a commission if you make a purchase. These are disclosed in the relevant articles and in our Disclaimer.
  • Sponsored Content: Any article written with sponsorship or compensation is labeled "Sponsored" at the top. Editorial conclusions are our own.
  • Free Access: We sometimes receive free access to tools for review purposes. This does not guarantee a positive review.

7. Privacy and Sensitive Content

We follow responsible reporting practices when covering topics related to personal data, security vulnerabilities, or sensitive hiring situations. We do not publish personal information about private individuals without their consent.

8. User-Generated Content

Currently, RapidFast does not host user-generated content or public comments. If this changes, this policy will be updated to reflect how such content is moderated.

9. AI-Assisted Writing Disclosure

We may use AI writing tools as research or drafting aids. However, all published content is reviewed, edited, and verified by human team members who take responsibility for its accuracy. We do not publish raw AI output without significant human oversight and editing.
