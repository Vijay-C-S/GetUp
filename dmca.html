2. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe that content available on getupdated.tech infringes on your copyright, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice by contacting us at the address below.

Please note: We only accept infringement notices from the actual copyright holder or from someone legally authorized to act on their behalf. False claims of infringement may result in legal liability under the DMCA.

3. What to Include in Your Notice

To be valid under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), your notice must include all of the following:

  • Your full legal name and contact information (email address, phone number, and mailing address)
  • A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
  • The exact URL(s) on our website where the allegedly infringing content appears
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Important: Submitting a false DMCA notice is a serious matter. Under 17 U.S.C. Section 512(f), any person who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages. Please verify your claim before submitting.

5. Our Response Process

Once we receive a valid and complete DMCA notice, we will:

  1. Acknowledge receipt of your notice within a reasonable time
  2. Review the claim to determine whether it meets DMCA requirements
  3. Remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing content if the notice is valid
  4. Notify the uploader (if applicable) that the content has been removed
  5. Document the notice for our records

Incomplete or invalid notices will not be acted upon. We reserve the right to seek clarification before taking action.

6. Counter-Notification

If content you submitted to us has been removed following a DMCA notice that you believe was submitted in error or misidentified your content, you may file a counter-notification. Your counter-notification must include:

  • Your name, address, phone number, and email address
  • Identification of the removed material and its location before removal
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification
  • A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the courts in the jurisdiction where your address is located
  • Your physical or electronic signature

We will forward a valid counter-notification to the original complainant. If no legal action is filed within 10-14 business days, we may restore the content.

7. Repeat Infringer Policy

GetUp maintains a repeat infringer policy. Users or contributors who are found to repeatedly post infringing content will have their access to our platform permanently terminated.

8. Third-Party Content

GetUp may reference or link to third-party content in our articles. We are not responsible for content hosted on external websites. For infringement on third-party platforms, please contact those platforms directly.

9. Image Credits

Images used on GetUp are sourced from original production, licensed stock image providers, or publicly available press materials. If you believe an image on our site infringes on your copyright, please contact us and we will investigate and remove it promptly if necessary.
